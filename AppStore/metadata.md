## Version 1.0.0

### Promotional Text (≤170, can be changed without review)

Stay on track with your clear aligners: a live 2-hour timer on your Lock Screen, tray change reminders and practical tips for every day of treatment.

### Description (≤4000)

SmileTrack helps you wear your clear aligners the way your orthodontist intended — 22 hours a day, switching trays right on time.

2-HOUR DAILY LIMIT
• Tap once when you take your aligners out and once when you put them back in.
• A ring shows how much of today's 2 hours is left.
• See the countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with Live Activities.
• Get a heads-up 15 minutes before the limit and a reminder when it's used up.
• Review every removal today and your totals for the last 7 days.

TRAY SCHEDULE
• Enter your start date, number of trays and days per tray.
• See which tray you're on, when the next switch is and when treatment ends.
• Get a reminder on switch day at the time you choose.

TIPS FOR EVERY DAY
• What to eat and drink, and what to avoid.
• How to clean and store your aligners.
• How to handle pressure and discomfort after a switch.
• A new tip of the day on the main screen.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. All your data stays on your device.

SmileTrack is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your orthodontist. Always follow your treatment plan.
