# SmileTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

SmileTrack ("the app") is developed by Marat Faizerakhmanov. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information the app stores

The app stores only what you enter or create while using it:

- times when you took your aligners out and put them back in;
- your treatment plan: start date, number of trays, days per tray and reminder time.

This information is stored **only on your device**. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Information the app does not collect

SmileTrack does not:

- require an account or sign-in;
- collect your name, email, location, contacts, health records or any other personal data;
- use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind;
- share or sell any data.

## Notifications and Live Activities

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications (daily limit and tray change reminders) and shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen. These are created on your device and do not use any server.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual records in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Medical disclaimer

SmileTrack is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your orthodontist.
